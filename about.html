About the Program

A partnership between Koenig Solutions and V Group Inc. to upskill developers on the fastest-moving intersection in commerce right now: agentic AI on Shopify.

KOENIG solutions

Koenig Solutions

Koenig Solutions is a global IT training and certification provider with over 33 years in the industry, having trained close to a million certified professionals worldwide. It partners with 35+ technology vendors — including Microsoft, AWS, Cisco, Oracle, VMware and Red Hat — across 5,000+ courses spanning cloud, cybersecurity, networking, and AI/ML.

Training is delivered across seven formats (classroom, live online, on-site, self-paced, one-on-one, custom and webinar), backed by 300+ certified trainers and recognitions including 2025 Microsoft Training Services Partner of the Year and Red Hat Gold Partner of the Year. Koenig is designing and delivering this bootcamp's curriculum, labs, and live instruction for V Group Inc.'s developer team.

V Group Inc.

V Group Inc.

V Group Inc. is a digital commerce and IT solutions company specializing in web design, web development, and mobile app development — with deep expertise in e-commerce platforms including Shopify, BigCommerce, Magento, WordPress, and Drupal. It also builds proprietary Shopify apps and Magento extensions for online retailers.

V Group has delivered 350+ web stores for clients spanning 20+ industries, combining onshore presence with offshore delivery for cost-effective, locally-supported engagements. The company is ISO 9001:2015 certified and CMMI appraised — this bootcamp is part of its investment in its developer team's ability to design and ship agentic commerce experiences on Shopify.

Program Philosophy

Why workshop-based, not lecture-based

Agentic commerce is a fast-moving, hands-on discipline — reading about tool calling or MCP doesn't build the muscle memory that writing and debugging it does. This program is deliberately structured so concept time stays light and lab time stays heavy.

🎯

Build, don't just watch

Every module ends with working code against a real Shopify dev store — not a slide deck.

🧩

Compounding labs

Each lab builds on the last — by the capstone, you're extending a system you already understand end to end.

🛡️

Production mindset

Guardrails, safety, observability and deployment aren't an afterthought — they're their own module.
